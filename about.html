About Me & Session Work

Hi I am Saint Yves Mayol, you can call me Yves. I started playing the bass at the age of 15, from then on I started growing outwards. I'm also fun of different passions such as basketball, video editing, video games, audio and visual art.

I grew up in a house that played records instead of the radio, and most of what I know about timing. I could remember really having fun in discovering music such as one time me and my sister were sharing the same phone playing the same song and from then on I kept looking for music that sounds really good to me.

These days I split my time between writing my own material and playing bass for other people's — the second half is what fills most of my calendar, and it's the part below.
